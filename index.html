About

I'm an earth science researcher at IISER Kolkata working at the seam between geophysics, optimization, and data science — one week reading spectral signatures out of seismograms, the next building Gurobi models to route a supply-chain company's field staff. I like problems that force you to think in systems: rock layers, wave equations, delivery routes, probability distributions. Same instinct, different instrument.
